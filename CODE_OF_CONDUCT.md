# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a friendly, welcoming place for everyone, regardless of age, background, experience level, identity or nationality. It's a small hobby and educational project — kindness goes a long way.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive
- Welcome newcomers and answer questions patiently
- Give and accept feedback graciously
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people using it

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or imagery
- Trolling, spam, or deliberately derailing discussions
- Publishing others' private information

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues and pull requests that don't follow this Code of Conduct, and may block contributors who repeatedly violate it.

To report a problem, email **rajdipdharcode@gmail.com**. Reports are handled privately.
